## 3. Strategy shape

The working strategy has three layers:

```text
Heller-Godel apparatus
  -> bridge primitives
  -> Clay-grade Hodge target
```

The Heller-Godel apparatus supplies finite-character, Deligne-adjacent, regulator-local, and proof-fabric artifacts.

The bridge primitives, defined in `docs/scaffolds/hodge_bridge_requirements_scaffold.md`, require at minimum:

```text
Hodge target datum: (X, k, alpha)
cycle realization datum: algebraic cycles Z_i and rational coefficients q_i
cycle equality obligation: alpha = sum_i q_i cl(Z_i)
Deligne-to-Hodge bridge theorem: a theorem connecting HG data to the target datum
```

The Clay-grade Hodge target requires a nonsingular complex projective variety `X`, a rational Hodge class `alpha`, and algebraic cycles realizing the class.

## 9. Advancement conditions

The strategy advances materially only if at least one of the following is supplied:

1. a verified new proof-class analytic row that tests a new stratum;
2. a one-parameter or otherwise structured family of proof-class analytic objects;
3. transition or descent data between local analytic realizations;
4. a candidate analytic parameter object with explicit construction;
5. a candidate algebraic parameter object with explicit equations or represented functor;
6. a smooth complex projective target variety `X`;
7. a rational Hodge class `alpha`;
8. algebraic cycles `Z_i` and rational coefficients `q_i`;
9. a typed equality target in `H^{2k}(X,Q)`;
10. a theorem connecting Heller-Godel data to a Hodge target datum.

Absent one of these, the program remains at method-side registry organization.

## 10. Revision or abandonment conditions

The current strategy should be revised or abandoned if one of the following occurs:

1. proof-class analytic objects cannot be extended beyond the existing registry rows;
2. all plausible family-data attempts fail to produce transition, descent, or variation structure;
3. candidate parameter objects remain only discrete or stratified with no route to analytic or algebraic structure;
4. attempts to produce projective targets require assumptions stronger than the original problem;
5. Hodge bridge primitives cannot be typed from the apparatus without external unrelated constructions;
6. the framework repeatedly produces only restatements of missing Hodge data rather than new constructions.

A negative result under these conditions is still useful. It would show that the current Heller-Godel apparatus is a disciplined Hodge-method workbench but not a bridge to Clay-grade Hodge.
